POSITIONING DECISION
Private, household-specific care coaching for parents and the adults already caring for their children. Covers newborns, toddlers and older siblings, rather than only preparing a nanny for a baby. Make the nanny's professional perspective part of the work. Frame differences as a chance to clarify routines, preferences and responsibilities; do not market employee surveillance, performance scoring or a promise to resolve every family conflict.

Catalina's grounded differentiation comes from the existing resume's eight years of nanny, postpartum and household-care experience: newborn twins; infant/toddler transitions; a returning family with another newborn; daycare pickups and after-school activities; following parent/provider instructions. Do not invent a credential in adult instruction, therapy, mediation, HR, occupational assessment or professional certification.
